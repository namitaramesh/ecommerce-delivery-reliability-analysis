# Key Findings

## Overall delivery reliability

- The analysis includes 96,470 delivered orders with recorded customer-delivery dates.
- 7,826 orders were delivered after their estimated delivery date.
- The overall late-delivery rate was 8.11%.
- Median delivery time was 10.22 days.

## Geographic differences

- Among states with at least 1,000 delivered orders, CE had the highest observed
  late-delivery rate at 15.32%.
- BA followed at 14.04%, and RJ at 13.47%.
- SP had the largest delivery volume (40,494 orders) but a lower observed
  late-delivery rate of 5.89%.

## Order value

- Late-delivery rates increased across order-value bands.
- The lowest-value orders had a late-delivery rate of 7.18%.
- The highest-value orders had a late-delivery rate of 8.97%.
- Median delivery time increased from 8.87 days in the lowest-value band to
  11.33 days in the highest-value band.

## Product categories

- Electronics had the highest observed late-delivery rate among named categories
  with at least 500 order-category combinations: 9.81%.
- Baby and office furniture categories also had observed late-delivery rates
  above 9%.
- Category results use order-category combinations because one order can contain
  multiple product categories.

## Time patterns

- Late-delivery rates varied substantially across purchase months.
- The highest observed monthly late-delivery rate among months with at least
  1,000 delivered orders was 21.36% in March 2018.
- A previous increase was observed in November 2017 (14.31%) and February 2018
  (15.99%).

## Limitations

- This is a descriptive analysis of historical Olist delivery records.
- The analysis identifies associations, not causes of late delivery.
- Factors such as seller location, logistics routes, product mix, and delivery
  estimates may influence the patterns observed.
- Only delivered orders with recorded delivery dates are included in
  delivery-time calculations.
